# AI Qualification Guidelines & Fit Rubric

Leads are semantically evaluated against candidate profiles using the following scoring criteria:

1. **Location & Work Authorization**: Remote (Global, Worldwide, India-friendly) receives priority (80-100).
2. **Experience & YOE**: Early career (0-3 yrs) and high-agency founding roles preferred.
3. **Skill Alignment**: Adjacent AI Product, DevRel, and Full-Stack capability transfer.
